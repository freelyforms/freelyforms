# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues privately to **hello@freelyforms.com** with the subject line `SECURITY`. Do not open a public issue.

Include what you found, steps to reproduce and the potential impact. We aim to acknowledge reports within 3 business days and will keep you informed until the issue is resolved. We are happy to credit reporters who wish to be named.

## Scope

- The code in this repository.
- The hosted service at `freelyforms.com` and published forms at `freelyforms.link` (pre-alpha).

Please do not access other people's data, degrade the service, or use automated scanning that generates significant traffic. Abusive forms (phishing, spam) can be reported to the same address.
